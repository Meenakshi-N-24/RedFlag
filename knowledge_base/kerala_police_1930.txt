National Cyber Crime Reporting Helpline No 1930
August 03, 2023

National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal is an initiative of Government of India to facilitate victims/complainants to report cyber crime complaints online. This portal caters to complaints pertaining to cyber crimes only with special focus on cyber crimes against women and children. You can directly report financial crimes committed online and track the investigation progress of your complaint.

The National Cybercrime Reporting Portal (https://cybercrime.gov.in) allows direct reporting of online crimes, including financial frauds. You can register all types of online crimes through this portal. The helpline number 1930 is available 24 hours a day.

Reporting online financial scams promptly is crucial. It enables investigating officers to collect evidence and take necessary action before it gets destroyed.
